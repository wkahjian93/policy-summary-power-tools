# REDEFINE On Field: how to use it

On Field (formerly Policy Summary Power Tools) is one app for the field: record a client meeting by voice, send a personal follow-up PDF and WhatsApp message, keep every client in your farm, and analyse policies. The link is the same as before.

## Record a meeting (the main thing)

1. **Set up My profile once** (Settings, then 👤 My profile): photo, title, phone, email, "About me", "Why I do insurance". It goes on the last page of every follow-up PDF.
2. After a meeting, tap **🎙 Record a meeting** (home screen, or inside a client). Speak for 1 to 10 minutes (long notes are turned into text in 90-second parts, about 10 to 20 seconds each). If the connection drops, each part is tried 3 times; if it still fails, the recording stays saved on the phone and **Try again** sends only the missing parts, so you never need to speak again (or tap **Save the recording file** / **Use the text so far**), the way you would tell your leader: who you met, what they care about, what you explained, what you suggested, and the next step. Mix Malaysian Mandarin, Hokkien, Cantonese, English and Malay freely. You can also **Type instead** (or use the keyboard mic), and add photos of notes or drawings.
3. **Check the text.** Fix names and numbers. Hokkien and Cantonese words may be spelled oddly; the AI understands them, but numbers must be right. Pick the letter language (中文 / English / Bahasa).
4. **Draft follow-up.** The AI writes a personal letter in your voice, picks up to 3 REDEFINE concept pages that match what you discussed, writes the WhatsApp message, and fills in the farm record.
5. **Review.** Every number the AI heard is listed; tick each one after checking, and the PDF button stays locked until you do. Edit any text, add or remove concept pages.
6. **Create PDF & send.** The message is copied; share the PDF to the client in WhatsApp and paste the message. Or tap **Save only** to keep the record without a PDF.

**If sending fails, just tap Send again.** The PDF is kept on the phone the moment it is made, so nothing needs redoing: the send screen stays open when the app refreshes, comes back by itself if the phone reloads the app after WhatsApp, and every button can be tapped as often as needed. Each tap on Send copies the message again. On iPhone only the PDF goes to WhatsApp, so paste the message. If the share menu keeps failing, use **Save / open PDF** and attach it in WhatsApp, or **Open WhatsApp with the message only**. After tapping Done, the client's page has **📤 Send PDF again** (follow-ups in the farm timeline, summaries at the top) for 30 days on that device.

The follow-up PDF: a personal letter (what we talked about, what matters to you, my suggestion and why, next step), the chosen concept pages, photos of your notes, and an About me page with your name card. Concept pages come from the REDEFINE medical card and income protection lessons; their numbers are marked illustrative and they name no products.

## Client farm

Every client has a farm card: a **Before you meet** brief (last meeting, family and work, what they care about, concerns, agreed next step), a timeline of every meeting, and their details. Only names or nicknames are kept: no phone, IC or medical details. The AI is told to leave those out and the app also strips IC and phone numbers. Clients with a follow-up date in the next 3 days or overdue appear under **Follow up soon** on the home screen. With sync on, the farm syncs between your iPad and phone, locked with your passphrase.

## Sections (bottom bar)

- **📄 Summary:** every policy summary in one place. **New policy summary** asks who it is for (someone in your farm or a new name), then opens the Add policy pages tray straight away. Tap a name to see the summary and create the PDF.
- **🏠 Home:** Record a meeting, the **📄 Policy summary** shortcut (same as New policy summary), the follow-up summary card, and your people split into **⭐ Existing Clients** (policies you manage; their policy summaries live here) and **🌱 Prospects** (people you are approaching or following up). Each person has a bar to move them between the two. When a prospect closes, the app offers to move them to Existing Clients. Older records count as clients if they have policies, otherwise as prospects.
- **📋 Activities:** pick a day and see, separately, who you **Approached, Opened, Presented, Followed up and Closed**, with time, client/prospect and notes. **+ Add** logs an activity (date, name, type, notes) without recording. A person is copied forward to every stage they reached: an approach that got an appointment (O) also shows under Open, an Open that went to a presentation (P) also under Present, and a presentation or follow-up that closed also under Close. Each name appears only once per list.
- **🔁 Follow-ups:** everyone you present to is listed automatically with a Day 1 to Day 5 table (First to Fifth follow-up), days since the presentation, follow-ups done (x/5), and a red flag when someone is behind or has had no follow-up for 3 days. **Log follow-up** (WhatsApp, call or met), **Closed** or **Stop**. A new presentation starts the plan again.
- **3️⃣ 三访:** only face-to-face visits: today's count, V / R / month, the report, and the last 14 days. WhatsApp or call follow-ups count as activities but not as 三访 visits (untick "Face-to-face visit" when logging).

## Daily 三访 report

**While recording**, the record screen shows the REDEFINE guide (11 questions: time spent, what you did, CFF priorities, which stage had an issue (Relax–Disturb–Release–Close), income and cash flow, hot button, concept, plan and ANP, objections, how you handled them, next move). You don't have to cover all of them. The AI files your answers under each question in the client's farm, and you can edit them on the review screen.

**Each visit** also gets a time, O / F / P / R tags (O = appointment made, F = follow up, P = presentation, R = recruitment activity) and a short report note that the group sees. The AI suggests all three; tap a visit in the daily report to change them.

The home screen shows **三访 today** (people met out of 3) and this week's V and R against your targets.

1. In the evening tap **📋 Daily review & report**. Today's visits are listed in time order.
2. Met someone you didn't record? Tap **+ Add someone I met (quick log)** with type (including 了解需求 / Open and 招募 Recruitment), time, O/F/P/R and note. Choosing a type ticks the usual tag.
3. The report is in an editable box: change anything, then tap **Copy report for WhatsApp** and paste it in the group. Your edits are kept; **Undo my edits** rebuilds it from the day's visits. Tick **Hide client names** to show Client A, B, C.

The report follows the team format:

```
Rex👑 (SG Farlim)
以愉快的心情与大家一起修练三访。

13/11/2026 (Friday)

V: 06/18      visits this week / weekly target
R: 03/06      recruit visits this week / weekly target
Total: 24/72  visits this month / monthly target

第一访 9am (P)
Ms Koay
Direct approach, …
```

Set your report name, base, opening line and the three targets in Settings → 👤 My profile. Defaults: V 18 a week, R 6 a week, Total 72 a month.

**Your own headline and end line:** on the 三访 screen tap **✏️ Headline & end line**. The headline replaces the name and opening lines; the end line goes at the bottom of every report. Both can be several lines and can use `{name}`, `{base}`, `{date}` and `{day}`. If the headline has `{date}`, the separate date line is left out. **Reset** goes back to the team format.

## REDEFINE Calendar link

Connect once per device: Settings → **📅 Connect REDEFINE Calendar**, with the same team code, name and PIN you use in the Calendar.

- In a quick log, fill **Next appointment** (date and time). Saving puts a private 1-hour event in your Calendar ("Present 陈先生", "Follow up 陈先生" …) and the next-step screen shows ✅ In your REDEFINE Calendar.
- After the meeting, tap **✓ Done** or **💰 Closed** on that event in the Calendar. Next time On Field is opened (or within a minute if it is open), the visit is logged by itself in the farm, Activities and the 三访 report, with a toast. ✕ Didn't happen logs nothing. If you move the event in the Calendar, the new time is kept.
- Only the appointment title and time go to the Calendar. Disconnect any time in Settings; events already in the Calendar stay.

## After a close

When someone closes (logged here, from Follow-ups, or as 💰 Closed in the Calendar), the app shows the REDEFINE service steps, and their page keeps a checklist until each is ticked: **policy summary** done, **referrals** asked (opens REDEFINE Prospecting), **nominee** form (opens REDEFINE Form Filler), **e-Connect** set up for the client.

If **Share my numbers with my leader** is on (default), only the counts go to the team server. No client names.

**Leader view:** signed in with the owner code, the home card shows **👥 Team 三访**: each agent's today, this week, days at 3+, streak and closes.

## Backup & sync (phone, iPad, laptop)

Until it is on, the home screen warns: **Your clients are only on this device.** Tap **Turn on Backup & sync** once on each device, with the same name and personal passphrase.

- Everything is shared: clients, policies, meetings and guide answers, 三访 notes, your profile (photo, intro, report name, base, targets) and remembered benefit names.
- Phone, iPad and laptop can be open at the same time. Changes reach the others within about 30 seconds, and immediately when an app is reopened.
- If two devices change the same client before syncing, both changes are kept (meetings and policies are combined, a deletion stays deleted, and for the same item the newer edit wins).
- Deleted the app, new phone, or a new laptop: sign in, tap Turn on Backup & sync, enter the same name and passphrase, and everything comes back. Settings → Backup & sync → **Show my passphrase** shows it on any device where sync is on.
- Everything is locked with the passphrase before it leaves the device. A forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered by REDEFINE.

## AI limits

AI is shared by the whole team through the team server: about 300 AI reads a day across all REDEFINE apps, reset at midnight. Each group of up to 4 pages, each voice note and each draft is 1 read; up to 40 pages per upload. The app reminds agents of this on the upload tray and the record screen.

## Policy summary

**The pain:** a policy summary means gathering every policy from every insurer, working out what each one covers and adding it all up by hand. That takes hours.
**The fix:** snap or key in each policy, confirm anything the app wasn't sure about, and send the client a polished PDF in minutes.

**Where to find it:** the **📄 Summary** tab at the bottom, the **📄 Policy summary** card on Home, or the **📄 Policy summary** box at the top of any client or prospect page (totals, See summary & create PDF, + Add more policies).

### English / 中文

The summary screen and the comparison screen both have an **English | 中文** switch above the preview. It is remembered for each client (and syncs with them). The 中文 PDF uses the same layout in natural Simplified Chinese:
- Insurer, product and benefit names stay exactly as printed.
- Key terms keep their English short form, e.g. 严重疾病 (CI), 完全永久残废 (TPD).
- Short amounts read the way clients say them (RM30万, RM150万); full amounts stay as RM600,000.
- Dates are written 2026年11月23日. The file is named 保单总结 / 保单比较, and the WhatsApp message is in Chinese too.

English PDFs are exactly as before.

### ⚖️ Compare policies & proposals

1. On the client's page, tap **+ Add a proposal to compare**. The tray is set to **New proposal**: snap or upload the benefit illustration / proposal PDF (any insurer), or key it in and pick **Proposal (not bought yet)** at the top of the form. Proposals are listed separately and **never count in the policy summary**.
2. Tap **⚖️ Compare policies & proposals**. Tick 2 to 4 plans; the first one ticked (A) is the starting point and the others are compared with it.
3. If an existing policy is compared with a proposal, tick **The proposal replaces the existing policy** when it is a switch; page 2 then leaves the replaced policy out of the totals.
4. **Create comparison PDF**, then send it like the summary.

The PDF (same REDEFINE template):
- **Page 1, side by side:** death, TPD, CI, early CI, accident, medical (annual, lifetime, room & board), hospital income, premium waiver, premium and premium per year, paying term, cover until, cash value and bonus, riders. The highest amount in each cover row is shaded, each column shows its difference from A, and cover a plan does not have shows **Not included**.
- **Main differences at a glance:** neutral sentences (e.g. "Critical illness: RM300k in Proposal B vs RM100k in Existing A (+RM200k)"). It never says which plan is better. With 3 or 4 plans this box moves to page 2.
- **Page 2, your total cover:** today vs with each proposal (added, or replacing), with premium per year and the income guideline when income is known.
- **Plan details** for each plan compared.
- When an existing policy is compared with a proposal, every page carries a short note on what the client may lose by replacing a policy. Follow the replacement disclosure process before any switch.

The AI now also reads **cover to age / term**, **premium paying term** and which benefits are **riders**. Older policies can have these added in the form.

## Using it (about 5 minutes per client)

1. **Open the app** and tap **+ New client summary**. Enter the name as it should appear on the PDF. Age and annual income are optional; adding income turns on the "compared with a guideline" bars.
2. **Add policy pages.** Tap **Add policy pages** and add everything the client has, from any insurer, in any order. Use **Take photo** for paper pages, **+ Photos** for screenshots of insurer apps, and **+ PDF** for e-policies and e-statements. Keep adding until all policies are in, up to 40 pages. PDFs show every page with the first 4 picked; tap a page to leave it out. If a PDF has a password (e-statements often use the client's IC or date of birth), the app asks for it once and doesn't save it. Tap **Read with AI**. Pages are read in groups of 4, 3 groups at a time, and each PDF's pages are kept together. The AI keeps policies from different insurers separate, and parts of the same policy (same insurer and policy number) found in different groups are joined into one. You then see a list of the policies found; any pages that couldn't be read can be tried again. **Key in a policy** works offline with no AI.
3. **Confirm the orange items.** Anything the AI couldn't read clearly (a blurred amount, an unknown insurer, a benefit it couldn't place) turns orange. Fix it, or tap **Looks right**. **Missing amounts are never treated as RM0.** They show as "Not stated" until you fill them in.
4. **See summary & PDF.** You get three views: *At a glance* (the "wow" page), *Side by side* (every policy against every category) and *Details* (every rider).
5. **Create PDF**, then **Send to client…** The app asks you to confirm the client's name, then opens WhatsApp, email or another app. **Save / open PDF** keeps a copy.

## What the summary adds up

- **Death, TPD, Critical Illness, Early CI, Accident, Hospital income:** added up across all in-force policies. A "Death / TPD" benefit that pays once is shown under both, labelled "paid once".
- **Medical card:** shows the **highest** annual limit, the lifetime limit and room & board. Medical cards don't simply add up, and the PDF says so.
- **Cash value and bonus:** added up from the figures on the documents, with their dates.
- **"Drops to RM400k at age 65":** the app warns when term cover ends.
- **Lapsed, surrendered and matured policies** are listed in grey and not counted.

## Insurer and benefit recognition

The app recognises 24 Malaysian life and takaful insurers by full legal name, short form (PAMB, HLA, GELM…) or Chinese name. Benefit names are read in English, Malay or Chinese ("Penyakit Kritikal", "危疾", "Kad Perubatan"…). **When you change a benefit's category, the app remembers it.** The next photo with that benefit name is read your way.

## Use on iPad and phone (sync)

1. On the home screen, tap **☁︎ Sync off · Use on iPad & phone**.
2. Sign in with the team code if asked, then enter your name and a personal **sync passphrase** (8+ characters, not the team code). Write it down.
3. Do the same on your other device with the **exact same name and passphrase**. Your clients appear there.
4. After that it syncs by itself: when the app opens, when you save, and every minute while it is open.

Clients are locked with your passphrase before they leave the device. The server keeps only the locked copy, so other agents and REDEFINE cannot read them. A forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered (clients on your devices stay). The sample client is never synced.

## Privacy and safety

- Client details stay **on your own devices**. With sync on, they are also kept on the team server, locked with your passphrase. Photos are sent to the AI once, read and discarded (OpenAI `store:false`). Nothing is saved on the server.
- Policy numbers are cut to the last 4 digits. IC numbers are never kept.
- Before deleting a client or policy, and before sending, the app asks you to confirm.
- If some items are still unconfirmed (e.g. cash value not shown), you can still tap **Create PDF anyway**. The app asks you to confirm first. Missing amounts show as "Not stated" and are left out of the totals, and page 1 tells the client some details were not available.
- Every PDF has a disclaimer: it's a discussion summary from the client's documents, not a policy contract, quotation or recommendation.

## For maintenance

- `docs/` is the app. `logic.js` handles recognition and calculations, `report.js` builds the PDF pages, `app.js` runs the screens, and `config.js` holds the server address.
- `node test.js` runs 164 checks covering recognition, messy numbers, the never-guess rules, the summary maths, the 三访 report, activity stages, the Calendar link, the comparison and the 中文 PDFs.
- `node serve.js` previews the app at http://localhost:5193.
- `samples/` holds the sample PDF made by the app.
- `node test-sync-server.js` runs the real team server code in memory on port 5194 to test sync (open localhost:5194 and 127.0.0.1:5194 as two devices; team code `test-team-code`).
- The server is `REDEFINE Form Filler/server/Code.gs`. Both apps share its daily AI limit (`DAILY_LIMIT`).
